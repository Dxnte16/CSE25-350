About GET FLY

GET FLY is Gaborone's trusted reselling platform for youth who want real designer and streetwear labels without retail prices.

We verify authenticity and curate hoodies, pants, and kicks from top brands. Our goal is to make good streetwear easier to find in Botswana.

Whether you are shopping for campus, weekends, photoshoots, or everyday style, GET FLY helps you stay fresh for less.
